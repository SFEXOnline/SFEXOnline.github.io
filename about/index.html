WHY WE EXIST

THE LOVE NEVER
WENT ANYWHERE.

Seeing people still care about Street Fighter EX almost 30 years later has been genuinely special. The problem is that the scene has often been scattered, overlooked and difficult to find.

The EX games stayed tied to older PlayStation hardware and never received the same visible modern revival as many other classic fighting games. Along the way, EX picked up a strange reputation too. Some people remember the music and characters. Some see the blocky polygons. Others know there is far more hiding underneath.

We see the hype, the joy and the amount of tech still waiting to be explored. SFEXOnline exists to make that easier to see.

BEFORE SFEXONLINE

THIS DIDN’T START WITH US.

People were playing, uploading, preserving, drawing, sharing tech and talking about Street Fighter EX long before SFEXOnline existed. We want to recognise that history, credit people properly and give their work somewhere easier to discover.
