Kairi only needs one bar to ruin your life.

Air fireball super, air kick super, ground super or a one-bar Raging Demon: once Kairi has meter, every opening becomes dangerous.

02 • CHARACTER GUIDE

WHY PLAY KAIRI?

BEST FOR: Players who are familiar with shoto characters but want strong air options, mobility and dangerous meter conversions.

  • Familiar shoto tools with a twist
  • Big damage with only 1–2 bars
  • Overhead knee can cancel directly into two different supers
